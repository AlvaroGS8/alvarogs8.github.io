I have loved video games since I was a kid, especially what they bring to people. I wanted to make them, and with a more technical profile I found programming. What I enjoy most is finding out how things work in games and, above all, making them work.

I want to keep digging into game AI: classic approaches like behaviour trees and custom systems, and maybe small neural networks for more believable enemies. I'm also interested in online gameplay, not running servers but the game side: replicating things and learning techniques like prediction. And I like gameplay itself, with mechanics and small systems such as custom vision detection.

I like working in a team and splitting the work by need, taste and skills. I also like to parametrize what I program, especially when there are designers, so they can tweak everything they want within limits that keep my systems from breaking.
